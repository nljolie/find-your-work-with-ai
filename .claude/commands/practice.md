# /practice: a practice interview

Ask which job in `jobs/` she wants to practice for. Read its `posting.md` and her files in `me/`.

1. Tell her how it works: five questions, one at a time, she answers in her own words, typed or spoken.
2. Ask five questions: two about her experience, one about a problem she solved, one about why this job, one about how she uses or would learn new tools like AI.
3. After each answer, give two quick notes:
   - One thing that landed.
   - One way to make it stronger, using a real story from her proof list.
4. At the end, give her three lines she can keep: her best story, her best one-line answer about AI, and one question she can ask them.

Save it as `jobs/<that job>/practice.md`.

Keep it encouraging. This is a rehearsal, not a test.
