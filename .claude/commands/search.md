# /search: real openings, scored and fitted

Read everything in `me/` first, especially "My path" in `me/what-i-want.md` and `me/my-work-history.md`. If `my-work-history.md` is empty, fill it first: ask her for her last three to five jobs or roles (paid or volunteer), dates if she knows them, and what she did. Or ask her to drop her resume into `me/` and read it.

## 1. Find openings
- Ask her where she lives and if remote work is okay.
- Ask her to paste a job link or the job text, or name the job titles from her path and search for openings with web search if you have it.
- Never visit a site that blocks automated reading. Ask her to paste the text instead.

## 2. Score each one, 1 to 5
For each opening, make a card:
- **Fit with what she loves** (from `what-i-like.md`)
- **Fit with what she has done** (from `my-work-history.md`)
- **Things that drain her** (from `what-drains-me.md`)
- **Overall: 1 to 5**, with one sentence on why.

Below a 3: say so kindly and suggest skipping it.

## 3. Fit her resume to the job
For a job she wants to try:
- Create `jobs/<company>-<job-title>/`.
- Save the job text as `posting.md`.
- Write `resume.md` using only her real history. Put the experience that matches this job first. Use the job's own words where they truly fit her.
- Write `cover-letter.md`, short, in her voice: who she is, why this job, one story from her proof list.
- Never add a skill, title, date, or number she did not give you.

## 4. Track it
Keep `jobs/tracker.md` as a simple table: company, job, score, date applied, status. She tells you when she applies. You never apply for her.

End each run with the next small step, one line.
