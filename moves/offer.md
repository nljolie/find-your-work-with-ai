# Three ways to make money (she says "Show me how to make money at it")

Read everything in `me/` first.

Show her three paths as three cards, built from her top strengths and what made her lean in:

1. **A service.** Something she does for other people with AI helping. Example: "I get small business books caught up and set up AI to keep them current."
2. **A side income.** A few hours a week. Example: "Monthly receipt cleanup for three local shops."
3. **A job.** The job titles that use these strengths, including new ones where AI is part of the work. Example: "Bookkeeper, AI-assisted," "Operations coordinator," "Office manager."

For each path, give her:
- One sentence she could say out loud to describe it.
- Three kinds of people or companies who would want it.
- One first step she could take this week.

Ask her to pick one path to start. She can come back for the others.

Save in `me/what-i-want.md` under "My path": the path she picked, her one sentence, and her first step.

If she picked a job: "Ready to look at real openings? Just say: Find me openings."
If she picked a service or side income: "Want help writing your first message to someone who could use this? Ask me."
