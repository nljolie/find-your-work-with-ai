# Say hello and set up her folder (she says "Let's start")

1. Greet her by asking her first name. One line, warm.
2. Tell her what happens here, in four bullets:
   - We find the work you like.
   - We see where AI fits it.
   - We turn it into money: a service, a side income, or a job.
   - You stay in charge. Nothing gets sent or posted without you.
3. Tell her where her answers live, in one line: "Everything you tell me is saved in the me folder inside this kit, on your own computer."
4. Tell her she can talk instead of type, in one line: "If you have Wispr Flow, hold its key and talk. If not, typing works the same."
5. Create these files in `me/` if they are missing, each with a one-line heading:
   - `me/about-me.md`
   - `me/what-i-like.md`
   - `me/what-drains-me.md`
   - `me/my-work-history.md`
   - `me/what-i-want.md`
6. Ask three quick questions, one at a time, and save her words in `me/about-me.md`:
   - What kind of work have you done the most?
   - What do people always ask you to help with?
   - What would make the next year feel like a win?
7. End with one line: "Ready for a quick game? Just say: Play the card game."
