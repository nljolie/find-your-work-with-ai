# The card game (she says "Play the card game")

Goal: find the work she loves, the work she is good at, and the work that drains her. Make it feel like play.

## Round 1: Love it, fine, or no thanks
Show one card at a time. She answers **love it**, **fine**, or **no thanks**. Keep a running score and show it every five cards.

Start with these eight cards, in this order. They are the same cards as the game on the Find Your Work page, so if she played it there, ask her if she remembers her picks and use them.
- Organizing, decluttering, or cleaning up an office, a file, a house, or whatever you like to do.
- Balancing your checkbook, managing your money, investing, bookkeeping, or setting a budget.
- Solving puzzles, playing games, or creating something. You like figuring out how the pieces fit together and building on an idea.
- Planning birthdays, weddings, business events, talks, group trips, or family get-togethers. You enjoy choosing the place, working out the details, and bringing people together.
- Being the person people turn to with questions. You enjoy finding the right answer, explaining it clearly, and helping someone find what they're looking for.
- Making complicated things simpler. You enjoy breaking a task into clear steps, finding an easier way to do it, and helping someone work through it.
- Playing detective to find an answer you can trust. You enjoy comparing sources, checking what holds up, and asking better questions until you have enough to make a clear choice.
- Turning what you've found into a plan people can use. You enjoy putting the steps in order, making the plan manageable, and showing someone how to put it into action.

Then keep going with these (shuffle them):
- Organizing a messy space or file
- Keeping the books balanced
- Doing taxes or paperwork
- Solving a puzzle
- Working with numbers and math
- Planning an event or a trip
- Keeping a team on schedule
- Answering customer questions
- Teaching someone a new thing
- Writing clearly
- Putting things together, building or fixing
- Making something look nice
- Taking care of people
- Researching and finding the answer
- Selling or persuading
- Making a process run smoother
- Tracking details nobody else notices
- Talking with people all day
- Working alone and focused

Add cards from her `me/about-me.md` answers.

## Round 2: Her top five
From every **love it** card, ask her to pick her top five. Show them as a numbered list.

## Round 3: Proof
For each of the top five, ask one question: "Tell me a time you did this well." Save a one-line version of each story.

## Save it
- `me/what-i-like.md`: top five with her one-line proof stories.
- `me/what-drains-me.md`: every **no thanks** card.

End with a short celebration of her top five, in her words, then: "Next, let's see where AI fits. Just say: Where does AI fit me?"
