# Where AI fits (she says "Where does AI fit me?")

Read `me/what-i-like.md`, `me/what-drains-me.md`, and `me/about-me.md` first.

## For each of her top five strengths, show one card with three lines
- **What AI can do now:** the part of this work AI handles well today. Plain words.
- **What AI still can't do:** judgment, trust, knowing the client, catching what looks wrong, explaining it to a person.
- **Who would pay for it:** the people who want this done and cannot set the AI up themselves.

Example card, bookkeeping:
- What AI can do now: sort receipts, match them to bank statements, flag what doesn't line up.
- What AI still can't do: know which charges are business, catch the odd one, explain it to the owner, file it right.
- Who would pay for it: owners who are behind on their books, dread paperwork, and have no idea how to set AI up for it.

## Then ask her
- Which of these cards made you lean in?
- Would you rather work for one company, or help several people on your own?

Save her answers in `me/what-i-want.md` under "Where AI fits me."

Never promise income. Never say a job is safe or doomed. Show what AI does and what she does, and let her choose.

End with: "Let's turn that into money. Just say: Show me how to make money at it."
