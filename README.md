# Find Your Work With AI

You are good at something. AI can help you find out what, and who will pay for it.

This is not a job board. It is a place to figure out the work you like, where AI fits in, and how to make money at it. Then, if a job is what you choose, it helps you go get one.

Made by Nicole Jolie. Owned by Be Socially Smashing LLC. sociallysmashing.com/find-your-work

---

## Who this is for

- You are 35, 40, 50, 70, and you are good at real work.
- Organizing. Bookkeeping. Solving problems. Putting things together. Keeping people on track.
- You keep hearing AI is taking that work.
- You are not sure what comes next, or where AI fits.

Most business owners have no idea how to set AI up for their own books, files, or schedules. You already know how the work is supposed to go. That is the part they pay for.

## Set it up in four steps

### 1. Get the Claude app
- Download it at https://claude.com/download (the official link) and sign in. Mac, Windows, and Linux.
- Working with a folder on your computer comes with a paid Claude plan.

### 2. Want to talk instead of type? Add Wispr Flow
- Get it at https://wisprflow.ai/r?NICOLE342 (affiliate link).
- In Wispr Flow, click **Settings**, then **Data and Privacy**, and turn off **Dictation cloud storage**. Then Wispr does not keep your dictation on its servers. Logged out and back in? Do these steps again. Logging out turns cloud storage back on.
- Hold the fn key, talk, and your words appear. Typing works the same if you skip this.

### 3. Download the kit
- On sociallysmashing.com/find-your-work, click **Get your copy of the kit**.
- Double-click the file to unzip it. You get a folder called `find-your-work-kit`.

### 4. Make a project. Say Let's start.
- In the Claude app, under the message box, click **Project or folder**, then **New project**.
- Give it a name, like My AI Work Project.
- Click **Use a folder** and pick the `find-your-work-kit` folder. Do not upload the files. Use the folder.
- Click **Create project**.
- Claude asks to use the folder. Click **Allow**. Your Mac asks too. Click **Allow** again.
- Say: **Let's start.** The game begins.
- Coming back later? Open **Projects** and click your project. Your folder is still there.

## What you say, and what happens

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| Let's start | Claude says hello and sets up your folder |
| Play the card game | Pick the work you love, skip what drains you |
| Where does AI fit me? | What AI does, what you do, and who would pay for that help |
| Show me how to make money at it | A service, a side income, or a job |
| Find me openings | Real openings, scored for fit, with a resume fitted to each one |
| Practice an interview | Five questions, one at a time, with notes |

Any words work. Say it your way.

---

## What stays private

- Everything you share is saved in the `me/` folder inside the kit, on your own computer.
- Claude reads your files to work with you, so they are processed on Anthropic's servers, the same as any Claude chat.
- Nothing is posted or shared unless you do it yourself.
- Claude never makes up experience for you. It only uses what you tell it.
